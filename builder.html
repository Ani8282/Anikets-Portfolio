THE PROBLEM I CARE ABOUT

Passion is everywhere.
Opportunity is harder to find.

My early experience around gaming and tournaments showed me how much effort goes unseen when organisers lack tools, players lack structure, and local events struggle to grow. Sanity Gaming, the company behind Toornify, has evolved through several pivots while staying focused on making grassroots competitions easier to organise and easier to join.

The platform brings tournament tools and participation pathways together, with a focus on organisers and players who are often overlooked.
